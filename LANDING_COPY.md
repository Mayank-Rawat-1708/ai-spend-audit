## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
No. You enter your tools, get your audit, and only provide an email if you want a copy of the report.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
Every number is pulled from official vendor pricing pages, verified with a source URL and date. We update pricing when major plans change.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
Your email and company name are never shown on the public share URL — only tool names and savings figures. We don't sell your data.

**Q: What is Credex and why did you build this?**
Credex is an AI infrastructure credit marketplace — we source discounted Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, and API credits. StackAudit is free because it helps both sides: you find overspend, and if you do, Credex is the solution.

**Q: Can I share my audit results?**
Yes — every audit gets a unique public URL. The public version strips your email and company name but shows the full tool-by-tool breakdown.
