# Unit Economics

## What a converted lead is worth
Conservative ACV: $2,000. Optimistic: $8,000. **Assumption: $3,500 ACV.**
Credex margin: ~20%. Gross profit per customer: **$700**.

## CAC by channel

| Channel | Monthly Users | Email Rate | Consult Rate | CAC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Show HN (one-time) | 200 | 25% | 8% | ~$0 |
| X threads | 50/mo | 20% | 8% | ~$0 |
| Credex customer list | 100 (warm) | 40% | 15% | ~$0 |
| SEO (6mo lag) | 500/mo | 15% | 5% | Infra only |

All channels $0 paid. CAC = time + ~$50/mo infra.

## Conversion funnel
```
Visitors → Audit:        40%
Audit → Email:           25%
Email → Consultation:     8%
Consultation → Purchase: 40%

1,000 visitors → 400 audits → 100 emails → 8 consultations → 3 customers
Revenue: 3 × $3,500 = $10,500 ARR
Revenue per visitor: $10.50
Revenue per audit: $26.25
```

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 months
Need 286 customers → 715 consultations → 8,938 emails → 35,750 audits → **89,375 total visitors** (~5,000/mo).

5,000/mo is achievable: Show HN 500 + X 500 + SEO 2,000 + Credex list 500 + referral loop 1,500.
